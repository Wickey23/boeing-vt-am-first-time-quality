# ORIENTAI — coordinated service stress, displacement and steady temperature

## User experience
In the Engineering Copilot, upload and confirm a real STL, open **Simulate**, check the single physical-assumptions acknowledgement after reviewing elastic and thermal inputs, and click **Run All Applicable Analyses**.

The application runs:
1. **Service-load linear-elastic voxel solve on the original part axes.** One solve produces both von Mises stress and displacement vertex fields. The current service model clamps an entire extreme face and applies a total force to the opposing end; this is not detailed fixture/bolt/contact analysis.
2. **Steady-state solid thermal conduction solve** on the original part axes. This uses the approved conductivity, prescribed global lower-face temperature and upper-face inward heat flux; other surfaces are insulated. It is not transient metal-AM thermal history.
3. In the **Results** stage, choose the 3D **Service stress**, **Displacement**, **Steady temperature** or **Geometry** overlay. Contours are computed and rendered locally on the uploaded geometry, not generated by a language model.

The solvers work with a part whose selected manufacturing process is **PBF_LB_METAL**, **DED_LB_WIRE** or **DED_LB_POWDER**. They do not produce DED toolpaths, support strategies, process-specific residual stresses, fatigue allowables, print deformation, or manufacturing qualification. LPBF orientation screening remains a separate optional workflow and no longer blocks service computations.

## Review and safety
- Numerical force, fixture direction, elastic properties, conductivity, temperatures, heat flux and model resolution must all be inspected and approved together. Defaults are **illustrative** rather than Boeing or qualified aerospace data.
- Changing numerical inputs revokes approval and clears any outdated displayed numerical fields. Different part bytes, units or manufacturing process also invalidate local fields.
- The local job ledger stores the STL SHA-256 identity, approved inputs, reviewed source excerpts, model scope, executed steps, warnings and numerical summaries. It supports cancellation, failure, interruption and JSON export. Contour arrays are not persisted and must be recomputed after reload.
- Structural and thermal workers operate on the original part coordinate frame, with **identity rotation** regardless of print process. Rigid print orientation alone does not change isotropic service-load stress.
- Results may fail for open, disconnected, degenerate or under-resolved meshes, or numerical nonconvergence; failures are not silently marked as completed. Partial structural output may remain visible after a thermal failure and is not labeled a complete study.

## Validation
Tests cover direct two-stage job records for LPBF and both DED variants; browser E2E covers the single approval and run, the three overlay controls, result ledger export and reload, thermal-input edits and zero-force blocking. Run the standard engineering gate: \`npm test\`, \`npm run typecheck\`, \`npm run build\`, \`npm run test:e2e\`.

## Not part of this release
Automatic derivation of loads or thermal boundary conditions from PDFs, selectable constrained mesh surfaces, calibrated directional material/heat-treatment properties, thermomechanical print simulation, stress convergence across a documented mesh refinement study, and aerospace qualification.
