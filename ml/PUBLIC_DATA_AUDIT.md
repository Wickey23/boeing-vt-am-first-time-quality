# Public data qualification audit — 2026-10-15

## Model target
The currently implemented predictor targets **slicer-reported support volume (mm³)** from the same part and orientation as geometric features. A real label must be generated by a specified slicer and locked material/machine/support profile. A measured tensile property, melt-pool width, support proxy, or residual stress is **not** a valid label for this model.

## Current release gate
- No matching publicly retrieved, verifiable support-volume labels for multiple distinct metal parts and orientations.
- No production model trained or released from the above sources.
- Next: obtain process-matched, per-orientation slicer support volumes and original STL source geometry, with at least five distinct parts and 30 observations for the baseline exploratory training script; substantially more independent components and rigorous holdout validation will be needed for reliability.
- Never invent target labels, use the computed geometric support proxy as truth, or assert qualification from public research data.
- Raw source links above are documentation and research leads, not ingested training records.
